Hello, I am Ayesha

I photograph people the way they are when nobody is posing them: a grandmother laughing at the end of a long dinner, a groom fixing his cuff, a child asleep on a shoulder. My work leans on soft, natural light and on patience.

Soora is my studio for portraits, weddings and family stories. Every session is small and personal. I plan it with you, guide you gently on the day, and edit each image by hand.
