Ahlan, I am Ayesha

I picked up my first camera in 2006 and honestly, I never put it down. Since then it has travelled with me through Pakistan, the UAE and now Saudi Arabia, to birthdays, weddings, quiet family afternoons and busy brand shoots.

Photography has never felt like work to me. I still get that little flutter of excitement every time I arrive at a shoot. I love natural light, the way it warms the skin and keeps colours true, and I love the small unplanned moments: a laugh that slips out, a hand reaching for the cake, a glance nobody else noticed.

Once the shoot is over, I sit with every single picture myself. I soften the skin, brighten smiles and take care of the little details, always making sure you still look like you. Then I deliver your photos the way you need them, ready to print, to share online or to tuck into the family album.
